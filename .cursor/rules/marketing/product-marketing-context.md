# Product Marketing Context — Expiration Reminder AI

## Product

**Name:** Expiration Reminder AI
**URL:** https://expirationreminderai.com
**Tagline:** Never miss a critical deadline again.

**What it does:** AI-powered document tracking platform that extracts expiration dates, renewal clauses, and notice periods from uploaded documents (contracts, licenses, insurance, certifications, permits). Also supports manual deadline entry for any document type.

**Key features:**
- AI extraction from uploaded PDFs/images (OCR + Gemini AI)
- Manual deadline/reminder entry
- Cancel-by date calculation
- Automated email alerts (30, 14, 7 days before deadline)
- Color-coded dashboard (safe / approaching / urgent)
- AI confidence scores and clause previews
- HIPAA-compliant infrastructure

## Audience

**Primary ICP:**
- Healthcare professionals (medical licenses, DEA, malpractice insurance)
- Small business owners (vendor contracts, business licenses, leases)
- Legal professionals (bar licenses, client contract deadlines)
- Construction/contractors (bonds, insurance, equipment certs)
- IT teams (SSL certs, domain names, software licenses)
- Property managers (tenant leases, building permits, inspections)

**Buyer persona:** Busy professional who manages multiple documents with expiration dates and can't afford to miss a deadline. Currently tracking in spreadsheets, calendars, or memory.

## Positioning

**Category:** Deadline tracking / Document management SaaS
**Differentiator:** AI-powered extraction (upload and forget) + HIPAA compliance
**Competitors:** Due Date Radar, ContractSafe, Concord, manual spreadsheets
**Why us:** Only tool that combines AI document extraction with manual tracking, HIPAA compliance, and automatic cancel-by date calculation.
